# v0.0.4: the lessons

**Status:** built on branch `dev-v0.0.4`, waiting for the owner's check.

Six short lessons in a Lessons tab of the dock, for students who have never met a computer of 1987. The owner's aims: lessons students want to do; the CEC-I's own features in the light; a computer of forty years ago made familiar; our programs and the instruments put to work; and nothing long, complex or boring.

Related documents: the page's principles and decisions ([../docs/ui-design.md](../docs/ui-design.md), U13), the programs the lessons end with ([v0.0.3.md](v0.0.3.md)).

## 1. How a lesson goes

- **Short:** four or five steps, each a title and two or three sentences; a "Going further" aside, folded, for whoever wants more.
- **Doing, not reading:** a step has a chip. *Type it* types the step's line on the machine's keys (the visitor can type it themselves); *Show me* turns the 3D view to what the step is about, or puts an instrument (CPU & Memory, Screen, Chinese, Keyboard) beside the lesson, working; *Open* opens one of our programs.
- **A tick** once the machine shows the step done (the answer on the screen, the character drawn, the clock paused), in order: a step is checked once the ones before it are. The ticks and the lesson open are remembered.
- **A reward at the end:** every lesson ends with one of our programs to play.

## 2. The six lessons

| # | Lesson | Steps | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the CEC-I | one box and a TV (a photo, the front view); switch on (Ctrl-Reset, `]`); the TV is the screen (Display); look inside (the chips lit, the font chips; a photo of the board) | Snake |
| 2 | Talk to BASIC | `PRINT 2+2`; `PRINT "HELLO, CEC-I"`; a two-line program and `RUN`; Ctrl-C and `LIST` | Guess my number |
| 3 | Chinese inside | the 中文 key (no disk: the system is in the ROM); `PRINT` in Chinese; `LEN` of 中华学习机 is 15, three bytes a character (the Chinese tab beside); the font chips (Big character, the Chinese tab) | Reading numbers |
| 4 | Pictures from memory | `POKE 1029,193`: an A (the Screen tab beside); `GR`, `COLOR`, `PLOT`; `HGR`, `HPLOT`; Bar chart | Kaleidoscope (LOGO, built in) |
| 5 | Keys and sounds | the key matrix (the Keyboard tab beside); `MUSIC 128,110`; a scale in a `FOR` loop; Organ | Music box |
| 6 | Inside the processor | Pause and the registers (the CPU tab beside); 1/100 speed; real speed; BASIC against machine code | Clock |

What makes the CEC-I itself is in the middle: Chinese in the ROM, three bytes a character, the font seen through a window, CEC-BASIC's `MUSIC`, LOGO in the box. What makes a computer of 1987 is throughout: a TV for a screen, no disk, BASIC's prompt, line numbers, a byte that becomes a letter, a speaker that only clicks, a processor of a million steps a second.

## 3. How it is built

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| The lessons, as data: each step's keys, Show me, program, photo, check | `js/lessons/lessons.js` |
| Their words, in English and Chinese | `js/l10n/lessons.en.js`, `js/l10n/lessons.zh.js` |
| The Lessons tab: the bar, a lesson, its chips and ticks, the instrument beside | `js/app/lessons.js` (after the TRS-80 simulator's) |
| Show me: an instrument's content moved beside the lesson, and back when it is closed or its own tab is chosen (one instrument, one state) | `js/app/main.js` |
| A step's fresh start: Ctrl-Reset, BASIC afresh from LOGO's monitor | `System.typeFresh` |
| Start's "Begin the lessons" | `js/app/start.js` |

## 4. Checks

- Unit tests (`tests/lessons.test.js`): six lessons of four or five steps, each ending with a program; every word in both languages, every program and photo there; every lesson run end to end on a headless machine, each step typed (or the student's part done) and its tick checked.
- Browser checks (`tests/browser/lessons.mjs`): Start's button; Type it and its tick; Show me with the CPU tab beside the lesson, working, and back to its tab; Show me for a view; the next lesson's button.
