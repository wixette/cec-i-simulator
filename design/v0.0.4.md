# v0.0.4: the lessons

**Status:** built on branch `dev-v0.0.4`, waiting for the owner's check.

Six short lessons in a Lessons tab of the dock, in place of the Start tab, for students who have never met a computer of 1987. The owner's aims: lessons students want to do; the CEC-I's own features in the light; a computer of forty years ago made familiar; our programs and the instruments put to work; and nothing long, complex or boring.

Related documents: the page's principles and decisions ([../docs/ui-design.md](../docs/ui-design.md), U13), the programs the lessons end with ([v0.0.3.md](v0.0.3.md)).

## 1. How a lesson goes

- **Short:** four to six steps, each a title and two or three sentences; a "Going further" aside, folded, for whoever wants more.
- **Doing, not reading:** a step has a chip. *Type it* types the step's line on the machine's keys (the visitor can type it themselves); *Show me* turns the 3D view to what the step is about, or puts an instrument (CPU & Memory, Screen, Chinese, Keyboard) beside the lesson, working; *Open* opens one of our programs.
- **A tick** once the machine shows the step done (the answer on the screen, the character drawn, the clock paused); a step with a program, once it is typed in and started; a step with Show me alone, once it is shown. In order: a step is checked once the ones before it are, or once one of its own chips is used, so that a step skipped never holds the rest back. The ticks and the lesson open are remembered.
- **Typing is watched on the screen:** a step's Type it, or its program typed in, turns the 3D camera to the Screen view, from wherever it was (Inside, say).
- **Show me answers** every time: the camera flies to the view and the picture's edge glows once, even when the camera is there already.
- **A reward at the end:** every lesson ends with one of our programs to play.

## 2. The six lessons

| # | Lesson | Steps | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the CEC-I | one box and a TV (a photo, the three-quarter view; the history, folded); switch on (Ctrl-Reset, `]`); the TV is the screen (Display); look inside (the chips lit, the font chips; a photo of the board) | Snake |
| 2 | Talk to BASIC | three programs, each a step on from the last: two `PRINT` lines and `RUN`; the 7 times table with `FOR`/`NEXT`; a question with `INPUT` and `IF` (Ctrl-C, folded) | Guess my number |
| 3 | Chinese inside | the 中文 key (no disk: the system is in the ROM); `PRINT` in Chinese; `LEN` of 中华学习机 is 15, three bytes a character (the Chinese tab beside); the font chips (Big character, the Chinese tab) | Reading numbers |
| 4 | Pictures from memory | `POKE 1029,193`: an A (the Screen tab beside); `GR`, `COLOR`, `HLIN`: a yellow square; `HGR`, `HCOLOR`, `HPLOT`: an orange line and a blue; Bar chart; LOGO's turtle: `LG`, `FD`, `RT`, `LT` | Kaleidoscope (LOGO, built in) |
| 5 | Keys and sounds | the key matrix (the Keyboard tab beside); `MUSIC 128,110`; a scale in a `FOR` loop; Organ | Music box |
| 6 | Inside the processor | Pause and the registers (the CPU tab beside); 1/100 speed; real speed; BASIC against machine code | Clock |

What makes the CEC-I itself is in the middle: Chinese in the ROM, three bytes a character, the font seen through a window, CEC-BASIC's `MUSIC`, LOGO in the box. What makes a computer of 1987 is throughout: a TV for a screen, no disk, BASIC's prompt, line numbers, a byte that becomes a letter, a speaker that only clicks, a processor of a million steps a second.

## 3. How it is built

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| The lessons, as data: each step's keys, Show me, program, photo, check | `js/lessons/lessons.js` |
| Their words, in English and Chinese | `js/l10n/lessons.en.js`, `js/l10n/lessons.zh.js` |
| The Lessons tab: the bar, a lesson, its chips and ticks, the instrument beside | `js/app/lessons.js` (after the TRS-80 simulator's) |
| Show me: an instrument's content moved beside the lesson, and back when it is closed or its own tab is chosen (one instrument, one state) | `js/app/main.js` |
| A step's fresh start: Ctrl-Reset, BASIC afresh from LOGO's monitor | `System.typeFresh` |
| Start's content shared out: what the machine is and its first minutes to Meet the CEC-I (abridged), its keys and how Chinese goes in to References | `js/l10n/lessons.*.js`, `js/app/references.js` |

## 4. Checks

- Unit tests (`tests/lessons.test.js`): six lessons of four to six steps, each ending with a program, every step able to tick; every word in both languages, every program and photo there; every lesson run end to end on a headless machine, each step typed (or the student's part done) and its tick checked.
- Browser checks (`tests/browser/lessons.mjs`): the Lessons tab first on a first visit; Type it and its tick; Show me with the CPU tab beside the lesson, working, and back to its tab; Show me for a view, the stage's glow, and a step that only shows ticking; the next lesson's button, opening it at its top.
