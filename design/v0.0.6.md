# v0.0.6: ten languages

**Status:** released 2026-11-22 as a pre-release ([the release](https://github.com/wixette/cec-i-simulator/releases/tag/v0.0.6)).

The page in the TRS-80 simulator's ten languages, 简体中文 first.

Related documents: the page's languages ([../docs/ui-design.md](../docs/ui-design.md), U11), the choice of one ([decisions.md](decisions.md), D9), how Simplified Chinese is written ([../CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## 1. The languages

| Language | File | Its tag on the page |
|---|---|---|
| 简体中文 | `js/l10n/zh.js` | zh-Hans |
| English | `js/l10n/en.js` | en |
| 繁體中文 | `js/l10n/zh-TW.js` | zh-Hant |
| 日本語 | `js/l10n/ja.js` | ja |
| 한국어 | `js/l10n/ko.js` | ko |
| Español | `js/l10n/es.js` | es |
| Français | `js/l10n/fr.js` | fr |
| Deutsch | `js/l10n/de.js` | de |
| Italiano | `js/l10n/it.js` | it |
| Português (Brasil) | `js/l10n/pt-BR.js` | pt-BR |

Each has its lessons beside it (`lessons.<locale>.js`).

- **How they were made:**
  - **Simplified Chinese** is written in Chinese, not translated (U11).
  - **繁體中文** is adapted from it to Taiwan's own words: 程式, 記憶體, 螢幕, 磁碟, 位元組, 暫存器, 解析度, and 「」 quotes.
  - **The others** are translated from the English, with the Chinese beside it.
  - **Everything the machine types or shows stays simplified,** because the machine knows only GB 2312: the placeholder of Type in, `A$ = "中华学习机"`, the number reading on its screen.
- **Choosing one:**
  - The address's `?lang=` comes first, then the visitor's choice, then the browser's languages, then 简体中文.
  - Chinese is matched by its script and region: zh-Hant, zh-TW, zh-HK and zh-MO are traditional, any other Chinese simplified.
  - pt-PT goes to Brazil's Portuguese, fr-CA to French.
- **Fonts:** Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean pages put their own system fonts first, so that Han characters take their own shapes (`css/theme.css`).
- **The parts' labels:**
  - They move from the 3D model's English and Chinese into the messages, as `label.<part>` keyed by the part's name in the model. A key's label comes from its legend, `label.key`.
  - The model itself is unchanged.
  - The Chinese labels are written again from the parts' markings, which the English had already caught up with: U4, U5 and U6, for example.
- **One clash avoided:** where Display and Screen would be the same word (Spanish *Pantalla*, Italian *Schermo*), the Display menu takes another (*Visualización*, *Visualizzazione*).

## 2. Checks

- **Unit tests:**
  - Every language has every message, with English's placeholders, `code` and [[keys]] (`tests/l10n.test.js`).
  - The locale is picked as described above.
  - Every labelled part in the model has its message, and there is no message for a part the model doesn't have (`tests/model.test.js`).
- **Browser checks** (`tests/browser/lang.mjs`):
  - All ten open, each with its own title and tag, and with no message key showing in place of a message.
  - The language menu lists all ten.
- **By eye:** German (the longest labels) and Japanese on a desktop, and German on a phone, all fit.
